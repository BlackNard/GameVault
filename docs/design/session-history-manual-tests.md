## Persistence and audit

`Corrections/<correction-id>.json` sidecars retain stable game/session IDs, sequence, original measured duration, prior/new adjusted duration, reason and UTC creation date. Each sidecar is committed before its ID-based projection into schema-1 statistics adjustments. Interrupted projection is repaired on read; repeated recovery does not add time twice. Corrupt/future/recovered/missing/conflicting audits protect statistics and block further changes with diagnostics. Restore matching backups/compatible files rather than deleting audit records. Original session measurements, recovery journals and notes are never rewritten by a correction.
