GameVault development build

This wizard installs GameVault for your Windows account. Choose a writable local folder. The bundled .NET runtime does not require a separate runtime installation.

GameVault stores Data and Profiles beside its executable, including sessions, notes, covers and dated save backups. Upgrading and uninstalling preserve these folders. Keep a separate copy before moving an installation. Reinstall into the same folder to reuse retained data.

Quit GameVault from its tray menu before upgrading or uninstalling. Closing its window may leave it running in the tray. Setup will not stop a game or force GameVault to quit.

This unsigned development installer has automated build checks. Final installation, upgrade, uninstall, real-game and visual acceptance are still pending. This build is not a public release.
